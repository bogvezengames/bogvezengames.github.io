UFO DRIFT: Space Arcade

Privacy policy

Updated: 3 November 2026 · Android app: com.parvan.ufodrift

This policy describes the current internal test version of UFO DRIFT, which uses Google demo advertisements. It does not state that production advertising consent has been implemented. The policy will be reviewed before a public release.

1. Developer and contact

UFO DRIFT is provided by Bog Vezen. Contact for support and privacy questions: bogvezen@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The game stores your personal best, rank progress, selected ship, audio and vibration preferences, tutorial status and advertising-frequency settings locally on your device. It does not offer game accounts, a developer-operated score server, a global leaderboard or cross-device synchronization. Android app backup is disabled for the current Play test version.

You can remove this local data through Android’s app storage settings. Clearing storage removes progress and settings irreversibly.

3. Advertising and Google services

The test app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK and uses Google’s demo advertising identifiers for interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. Demo advertising still communicates with Google and is not a promise of zero data collection.

Google’s SDK documentation describes collection and sharing of IP addresses (which may be used to infer approximate location), app and advertising interactions, diagnostics, and device or other identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Processing depends on SDK version, configuration and applicable settings. Google’s practices and retention are described in its Privacy Policy and Mobile Ads data-disclosure documentation.

The production consent and privacy-options flow remains under preparation. This policy does not replace any consent required by applicable law or platform rules. Advertising and consent behavior will be documented after configuration and verification for public release.

4. Score sharing

If you choose Share Score, the app generates a temporary image containing your score and game details and opens Android’s sharing menu. You select the receiving app. UFO DRIFT does not publish automatically or upload the image to a developer-operated server. Receiving services process content under their own policies. Temporary images are kept in the app’s cache, which can be cleared in Android settings.

5. Permissions and diagnostic reports

The app uses internet access for advertising, network-state information and optional vibration. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location access. The advertising SDK may declare advertising-related permissions.

Diagnostic reports are displayed at your request. Copy report places the report on your clipboard; the game does not automatically send it to the developer. If you choose to email a report, it may include your phone model, operating-system and app versions, gameplay events and technical measurements. Please avoid including unnecessary personal information.

6. Support communications

If you email support, your email address and the content you send are processed to answer your question and investigate the issue. Email is handled through the support email provider, Google/Gmail. Support information is retained as needed to handle your request and any applicable legal obligations; you can contact us to request deletion of information you supplied. We do not operate a separate analytics or user-profile database for the game.

7. Retention, deletion and choices

Local progress remains until removed by you or the operating system. To ask about, access, correct or delete support information held by the developer, email the contact above. There is no game account to delete. Google and any sharing or email services may retain data under their own policies and legal requirements; we cannot promise deletion of data they hold independently.

Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights, including objection, restriction or complaint to a competent data-protection authority. Contact us with questions about information we hold.

8. Intended audience

The game is intended for teenagers aged 13 and older and adults. It is not designed or marketed specifically to children under 13. This audience statement does not override local protections for minors or advertising and consent requirements. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to support, contact us so we can review and address it.

9. This website

This static site is prepared for hosting on GitHub Pages. It contains no developer-added analytics, tracking scripts, advertising, embedded media players or contact forms. GitHub states that it logs visitor IP addresses for security when serving Pages sites. See the GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub Privacy Statement. Clicking an email link opens your email application; third-party links take you to services with their own policies.

10. Security and policy changes

We use available safeguards appropriate to the information handled. No service can guarantee absolute security. Google describes encryption in transit in its SDK documentation. This policy will be updated when the app’s features or data practices change materially.

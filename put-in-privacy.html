PUT IN: Perfect Timing

Privacy policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026 · Android app: com.putin.perfecttiming

This policy describes PUT IN's current Android test builds, which use Google sample advertisements. Google's privacy-consent integration is included in the app; activation and verification of the corresponding AdMob privacy message remain part of release preparation. This page does not announce a public game release.

1. Developer and contact

PUT IN is provided by Bog Vezen, the controller of personal information handled directly by the developer. For support or privacy requests, email bogvezen@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game stores your personal best, discovered heroes, tutorial state and sound preference locally on your device. Advertising and consent components may also store technical state and privacy choices. The game does not require an account, offer a public leaderboard, synchronize progress across devices or send scores to a developer-operated server. The developer has not added a separate analytics or player-profile service.

You can remove local app data through Android's app storage settings. Clearing storage or uninstalling the app removes locally saved progress and settings. Android app backup is disabled in the current test version.

3. Advertising and Google services

The app integrates Google Mobile Ads for optional rewarded advertisements and interstitial advertisements at natural breaks, and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for privacy choices. The current test build requests Google sample ad inventory. Test advertisements still involve communication with Google; they do not mean that no data is processed.

Google's SDK documentation describes collection and sharing of IP addresses (which may be used to infer approximate location), app and advertising interactions, diagnostic information, and device or other identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. UMP processes consent-related information to determine and manage applicable privacy choices. Actual processing depends on the SDK configuration, your region, device settings and applicable choices.

Google receives information directly through its services. Read Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and the Google Mobile Ads data-disclosure documentation for details.

4. Consent and advertising choices

The app checks Google's consent service before initializing advertising and requesting ads. Ad requests remain disabled unless that service reports that ads can be requested. This status is not, by itself, a promise that you have consented to personalized advertising.

Where Google's service requires a privacy-options entry point, a Privacy choices control appears in the main menu to let you revisit your selection. Which forms and choices appear depends on your location, the AdMob message configuration and Google's service. The app-side integration is present in the current test build; the corresponding AdMob message must still be activated and its behavior verified before public release.

You can also manage available advertising privacy settings on your Android device. Withdrawing a choice affects subsequent processing as applicable; it does not automatically erase data already held independently by Google.

5. Permissions

The app uses internet access and network-state information for advertising and related privacy services. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise-location access. Google's advertising components may declare advertising-related permissions and process advertising identifiers, subject to Android settings and SDK configuration.

6. Support communications

If you email the developer, your email address and the content you send are processed to answer your request and investigate problems. Email is handled through Google/Gmail. Information you voluntarily include, such as your phone model, app version or screenshots, may be used to diagnose the issue. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

The developer retains support communications only as needed to handle the request and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact the address above to request access, correction or deletion of information you supplied.

7. Retention, rights and international processing

Local progress remains on your device until you or the operating system remove it. Google manages retention and deletion of information processed by its services under its policies and applicable settings. There is no game account to delete. The developer cannot promise deletion of information held independently by Google.

Depending on your location and the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, request portability, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Email the developer about information held directly by the developer. You may also complain to your competent data-protection authority.

Google and the email and hosting providers may process information outside your country, under the safeguards and terms described in their privacy policies.

8. Intended audience

PUT IN is intended for teenagers aged 13 and older and adults. It is not designed or marketed specifically to children under 13. This statement does not override local protections for minors or advertising and consent requirements, and does not imply that the current test app verifies a player's age. Age-related advertising configuration remains subject to review before public release.

If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to the developer, email the contact above so the developer can review and address it.

9. This website

This static policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no developer-added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, contact forms or embedded media. GitHub states that it logs visitor IP addresses for security when serving Pages websites. See the GitHub Pages documentation and GitHub Privacy Statement. Email links open your email application; external links lead to services with their own policies.

10. Security and changes

The developer uses available safeguards appropriate to the information handled. No service can guarantee absolute security. Google describes encryption in transit in its advertising SDK documentation.

This policy will be updated when the app's features, advertising setup or data practices change materially. The effective date is shown above. The test-version statements will be reviewed before a public release.
